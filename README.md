Superstore Sales Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes a retail Superstore dataset containing approximately 9,800 transactions to identify sales trends, product performance, regional differences, and customer purchasing patterns.

The goal was to use Microsoft Excel to turn raw sales data into meaningful business insights and recommendations.

Tools Used

Microsoft Excel

PivotTables

PivotCharts

Excel formulas

Data visualization

Year-over-year growth calculations

Key Findings

1. Product Category Performance

Technology generated the highest sales at approximately $827,456.

Furniture generated approximately $728,659.

Office Supplies generated approximately $705,422.

2. Regional Performance

West generated the highest sales at approximately $710,220.

East generated approximately $669,519.

Central generated approximately $492,647.

South generated the lowest sales at approximately $389,151.

3. Customer Segments

Consumer customers generated approximately $1.15 million in sales.

Corporate customers generated approximately $688,494.

Home Office customers generated approximately $424,982.

4. Sales Trends

2016 sales declined by 4.3%.

2017 sales increased by 30.6%.

2018 sales increased by 20.3%.

Business Recommendations

Continue investing in Technology products due to their strong sales performance.

Investigate opportunities to improve sales in the South region.

Focus on retaining Consumer customers while growing Corporate and Home Office segments.

Investigate the factors contributing to the strong sales growth in 2017 and 2018.

Project Deliverables

Original retail dataset

Sales analysis by product category

Sales analysis by region

Customer segment analysis

Yearly sales trend analysis

Year-over-year growth calculations

Excel dashboard

Business insights and recommendations

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to organize, analyze, and visualize business data. The analysis helped identify important sales patterns and develop recommendations that could support business decision-making.
